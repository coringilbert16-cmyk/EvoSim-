# PLAN-005 — Cross-catalysis and cooperative chemical networks

**Status:** Planned; depends on PLAN-001 and controlled facilitation evidence from PLAN-002.

## Research question

Can two or more distinct molecular families facilitate one another's production or turnover, creating a network whose activity depends on interactions between pathways?

## Hypothesis class

A pathway may alter the availability, state, or reaction opportunity of a molecule involved in another pathway. The proposed relationship must be chemically specified and tested; merely observing two pathways in the same environment is not enough.

## Required evidence

1. Define at least two candidate pathways and the proposed cross-facilitation relationship.
2. Track exact molecule instances to distinguish facilitators from consumed substrates and regenerated products.
3. Compare matched conditions for pathway A alone, pathway B alone, and A+B together.
4. Normalize reaction rates and product yields for total inventory, reactant concentrations, geometry, contact opportunity, field exposure, and energy availability.
5. Show a reproducible combined effect beyond simply adding more reactants or reaction opportunities.
6. Account for all material and energy flows across the connected network.

## Controls and interpretation

Co-occurrence, shared intermediates, or higher total reaction counts in a larger mixture do not establish cooperation. The effect must be attributable to a defined chemical pathway and survive matched controls.

## Prerequisites

Complete PLAN-001 candidate reconstruction and establish a controlled facilitation methodology under PLAN-002. The current fixed-condition environmental workflow cannot yet execute the required paired conditions.
