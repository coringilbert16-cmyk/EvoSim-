## PLAN-004 — Pseudo-metabolism: initial pathway-turnover screen

### Question

Does the existing reaction history contain connected, temporally ordered transformations with repeated intermediate regeneration, beyond isolated reversible reactions?

### Observations

The five-seed dataset contains:

- 4,305 committed reaction events and 9,478 parent-child lineage edges.
- 158 composition/topology families and 426 distinct family-level pathway patterns; 178 patterns recur in at least two seeds.
- 4,200 distinct reaction-created molecule identities later reused as parents.
- 6,444 parent-lineage edges from reaction-created products, including 1,956 edges leading to COMBINE products.
- Four exact-instance BREAK→COMBINE fragment-reuse cycles already traced in PLAN-001: three N–O–P ⇌ N + O–P cycles and one H–O–P ⇌ H + O–P cycle, across seeds 43–45.
- Three further seed-45 sequences where H–O–P entered an H–O–P–S compound and was later released as a newly created H–O–P identity.

The recurrent reversible transformations are not confined to one seed. Across seeds 42–46, H + O–P → H–O–P occurred 16 times and the reverse 15 times; N + O–P → N–O–P occurred 13 times and the reverse 13 times.

### Interpretation

This is evidence of repeated intermediate-family turnover and reversible chemical interconversion. It is a useful starting substrate for PLAN-004, but it does **not yet establish pseudo-metabolism**.

In particular:

- The exact-instance examples show identity replacement during regeneration; they do not show one molecule identity persisting unchanged through a catalytic cycle.
- Family-level graph cycles can be generated by ordinary BREAK/COMBINE reversibility. A large strongly connected family graph is therefore not, by itself, evidence of a functioning metabolic network.
- The existing run starts with a finite inventory and has no recurrent external resource replenishment.
- The existing aggregate energy validation establishes run-level accounting, but does not allocate stored energy, heat, and losses to a proposed multi-step pathway.

### Outcome

**Promising pathway turnover found; pseudo-metabolism not demonstrated.**

The next PLAN-004 analysis should trace exact identities through longer connected sequences, prioritizing pathways that include more than one transformation stage and explicitly distinguish regenerated family from regenerated identity. A separate resource-fed experiment will be needed before claiming persistence under recurrent input. Do not repeat the generic baseline merely to extend its duration.

## PLAN-006 — Spatially organized chemistry: initial baseline screen

### Question

Do committed reactions show recurring spatial concentrations or zones that merit a controlled spatial experiment?

### Available geometry

The geometry-aware artifact contains 4,305 committed reaction events. Complete geometry for all participants is available for 2,811 events (65.3%); 1,494 events lack complete participant geometry. The analysis used the recorded parent-molecule world centroids to assign those 2,811 complete events to a descriptive 10×10 grid over the 250×250 world. Horizontal position is periodic.

### Observations

- 2,811 complete-geometry committed events were spatially binned.
- The most populated 25×25 region contained 448 events (15.9% of the geometry-complete subset).
- In seeds 42–45, the most populated individual bin was in the x=225–250 strip; seed 46's most populated bin was elsewhere.
- The fraction of geometry-complete events with x≥225 varied substantially by seed: 54.0% (42), 66.6% (43), 87.4% (44), 74.7% (45), and 14.0% (46).

### Interpretation

The descriptive distribution contains a possible spatial concentration, but it is not yet evidence that flow, resonance, or energy gradients caused a reaction zone.

This screen uses committed-event locations only. It has no complete spatial denominator for eligible reactant contacts, no local reactant-availability normalization, and no matched altered-flow/field condition. Unequal initial placement, movement, boundary effects, and missing geometry could explain the observed concentration. The strong between-seed variation also argues against treating the pooled concentration as a robust universal zone.

### Outcome

**Spatial heterogeneity observed; causal spatial organization not established.**

This is sufficient to identify a location pattern for follow-up, but not to run or interpret a controlled PLAN-006 intervention. The current workflow is baseline-only. A valid causal experiment still requires matched spatial conditions and adequate local opportunity/exposure denominators. No new measurement system is authorized by this result; first determine whether existing retained records can provide the missing comparison. Do not claim local reaction rates from committed-event counts.

## Shared limitations and accounting

- The five independent seeds, not individual reaction events, are the experimental replicates.
- Family labels describe constituent composition and bond topology; they do not encode bond energy, endpoint geometry, or full chemical state.
- Geometry is incomplete for 1,494 committed events and does not cover all eligible contacts.
- The EXP-2026-004 validation reported complete bond-pair attribution for all 4,305 events and passed per-seed inventory/energy validation. This supports run-level conservation/accounting, not pathway-specific energy-yield claims.
- No simulator behavior, reaction rules, thresholds, energy grants, or existing diagnostics were changed.
